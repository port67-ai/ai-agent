# AI research planning and evidence-backed medium selection

Owner authorisation: 16 November 2026, execute and deploy without approval pauses while owner is away. Extend CHG-0082; preserve existing runtime customer approvals, evidence controls, costs and permissions.

## Intent and selected approach

An LLM interprets the complete authorised public customer brief and selected channels, proposes a bounded research plan, and identifies provisional medium hypotheses. The task manager validates and versions this proposal; existing runners execute only selected tasks. After evidence and insight review, a second LLM pass recommends formats per channel with explicit evidence/confidence and mismatches against the requested deliverables. Producer confirmation determines actual production. No silent substitutions.

Rules-only routing is predictable but cannot interpret nuanced objectives. Letting an LLM directly execute arbitrary workflows gives away control. The chosen hybrid keeps the existing trusted capability catalogue, fixed dependency/approval rules, metering and durable project records while allowing the LLM to select researchers, questions and methods.

## Behaviour and boundaries

- New explicit work-type intake enables AI planning. Older projects remain compatible and have an explicit enable action before direction work. Planning uses an owner-selectable synthesis model slot, existing synthesis defaults and a versioned skill. It runs before any paid scope/research; a failed plan blocks research until explicit retry or an audited producer choice to use the rules plan.
- Planner receives all authorised public brief fields, requested formats, launch-channel playbooks, existing approved evidence when deliberately replanning, approved agent/method catalogue and available capabilities. Contact identity and raw logo data are excluded. It may acknowledge a supplied logo but cannot claim to have inspected it. Website URLs are leads, not browsed evidence.
- Strict structured validation requires exactly one decision per known research agent, unique selected foundational brand/customer roles, bounded questions/methods/page instructions, meaningful selection reasons, requested-output links, channel-specific preliminary medium hypotheses and capability-gap proposals. Unknown agents, arbitrary tools, malformed or extravagant plans are rejected with usage retained. Producer overrides remain explicit and authoritative.
- Research methods strengthen existing seven roles: public brand claims vs independent proof; product/offer facts; customer journey/review dates/denominators/brand-language echo; category maturity and alternatives; paid library vs measured outcomes; organic social baselines; adjacent transfer limits; dated trends; honest video provenance. Implement original Port67 guidance, not copied third-party runtime instructions or proprietary skills.
- After accepted insights, medium recommendations identify requested vs proposed format/channel pairs, supporting accepted sources/insights, counterarguments, confidence, unresolved questions and clearly labelled model/channel priors. General knowledge is not measured history. No private ad-account access or cross-customer learning is invented. A producer must record a reason when choosing a format that differs from the current recommendation/request or when proceeding without a recommendation.
- Planner can be explicitly rerun with evidence gaps before direction work. Revisions preserve compatible completed research and invalidate only affected researcher scope/results; stale in-flight outputs retain metering but are not accepted. No blind automatic retries. Existing post-direction locks remain.
- Catalogue expansion is selective and reviewable: planner proposes a missing capability with why current roles are inadequate and required evidence/tools; it is saved to the existing capability proposal backlog. It cannot install agents, grant tools or launch proposed capabilities. Product/market methods initially stay in brand/competitor roles; expand only on demonstrated gaps.
- Research-only journeys stop after reviewed insights, without paid creative medium selection. Existing projects without AI planning and legacy format/agent fixtures retain their contracts.

## Acceptance

Planner reads the full public brief but no contact/logo payload; invalid/unknown/duplicate agents and invented channel formats fail closed. A selected plan controls actual research invocation. Planner and medium calls have separate model/skill slots and paid ledger attribution; failures and stale outputs retain usage. Customer requested deliverables are immutable inputs, not rewritten by recommendations. Medium evidence is restricted to accepted supplied records, and unknown performance stays unknown. Changed relevant inputs invalidate recommendations. Producer decisions are audited/version-aware. Capability proposals never execute. API, unit, rendering/browser, budget/role/legacy regressions, typecheck, lint, build and CI pass before authorised merge/deploy.
